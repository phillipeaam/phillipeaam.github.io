About

My path into game engineering, how I work with teams, and what shapes my perspective.

I came to game engineering through mobile software development. Since then, I’ve worked with multidisciplinary teams on commercial games and interactive learning products.

Along the way, I earned the Unity Certified Developer credential in 2017.

Beyond implementation, I help translate product needs into technical plans, coordinate priorities and support other developers. I work with product and engineering partners to improve features and workflows using user feedback, and contribute to release preparation and maintenance.

Games have always been part of my family life: I grew up playing Super Mario World with my parents, still join them and my siblings in Tibia, and now play Super Mario Bros. Wonder with my son and wife.
