# Review report template

Use the structure below; fill it with evidence rather than generic commentary. For a batch, give each project its field checklist, dimension profile and recommendations; consolidate shared renderer defects once and identify all affected projects.

## Scope and sources

Project: name, stable ID and route. Kind: commercial / independent / jam / study. Audience/role: provided objective or general engineering readers. Reviewed state/date: identify current files/rendered state; include commit only if verified and distinguish local edits. Rubric: 1.0.0.
Sources: Source of Truth/report locators, current contracts and inspected surfaces. Distinguish technical evidence, personal account, product sources, interpretation and browser observation. List inaccessible sources/conflicts.

## Executive diagnosis

One paragraph: what this entry communicates effectively, principal limiting evidence/presentation issue, and useful next step. Do not equate a score with candidate seniority.

## Twelve-field checklist

Every row needs current value/absence, source/observation, adequacy/problem and a specific recommendation. Status vocabulary: Supported / Needs correction / Missing applicable evidence / Appropriate omission / Not observed. A field can be source-supported but not yet observed; state both.

## Dimension scores

All ten dimensions from rubric.md, each with the following columns. Use Not evaluated where needed, not a fabricated integer.

| Dimension | Score /10 | Confidence | Evidence locator and rationale | Main limitation | Improvement that could raise it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Optional overall: formula, weights, assessed coverage and missing dimensions. Separate any critical finding.

## Leading priorities and concrete proposals

Up to three highest-value priorities, followed by other justified findings. For each:
- Priority and observed issue, with evidence locator.
- Proposed change and English replacement text when support permits.
- Why it may improve comprehension/relevance; distinguish inference from research.
- Available source material versus evidence still needed.
- How to verify the change.

## Evidence gaps and necessary questions

Ask only for unresolved information that matters after searching available sources. Separate source-only opportunities, unrecovered historical evidence and ability that the entry cannot establish. Do not request commercially scaled outcomes for every project.

For targeted questionnaires and requested technology inventories, use [evidence-recovery.md](evidence-recovery.md). Present at most five initial questions with gap/dimension, useful-answer cues and evidence needs. Classify answers before proposing publication. Keep candidate technologies separate from public chips, and unpublished information separate from scored evidence. Interview-only responses do not require new scores or the full twelve-field report.

## Observed validation and limitations

| Route / link | Viewport or method | Actual observation | Result / limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

For external links: original/final URL, status when available, visible title/content, label/project/variant fit and Pass / Confirmed failure / Inconclusive. Include date/region when pertinent. For browser findings identify real versus emulated conditions. No physical-device/screen-reader/compliance claims from DOM or viewport emulation. Local loading does not establish production performance. Note applicable excluded tests without reinstating them as user action items.

## Readiness and reassessment

Editorial readiness category from rubric.md, reasons, and separate human approval state. No implicit approval or publication. Reassessment: prior rubric/version/state, score deltas and the new evidence responsible. Do not backfill historical observations or silently change calibration.
